### Current product

## Avance

**Avance** is a personal evolution and progress tracking platform.

Instead of being just another task manager, Avance focuses on showing the user's journey over time through:

- Goals and activities
- Steps and progress
- Work sessions
- Timeline and history
- Notes and reflections
- Time invested
- Progress indicators
- Reports and statistics

**Status:** Active development
